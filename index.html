💥 THE LAWS OF CHAOZ.IO

The Goal: Fly around, launch bouncy lasers, and avoid explosions! If playing Bomb Tag, tag other players to pass off the thermonuclear bomb before it explodes on your head!

Keyboard Controls:

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move and thrust in the arena
  • Mouse: Point and target players
  • Left Click / Spacebar: Shoot lasers
  • Right Click / Shift: Nitro speed burst

Video & Meme Splat Features:

Turn on your Webcam/Mic at the top bar to project your real-time video inside your player circle! Splat cool images onto the battlefield floor using the file picker.

This game runs completely peer-to-peer! There are no server delays, and chat is 100% direct and unfiltered. Soundboard buttons immediately blast audio straight to everyone's speakers. Go wild!
